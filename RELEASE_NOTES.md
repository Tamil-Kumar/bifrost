## Bifrost 0.40.0

- **The pointer in full screen:** after a YouTube video went full screen, the pointer stayed on it when the video's controls went away, until you moved it and waited again. Chromium shows the pointer of whichever page it last saw the mouse over, and only looks again when the mouse moves. Going full screen moves the window under a still mouse, and on Windows Chromium could take that as the mouse being over Bifrost's own window instead of the video, so the video's "no pointer" went nowhere. Now a page in full screen tells Bifrost when it wants no pointer, and Bifrost hides it over the video too.
- **Dark mode and Catppuccin, together:** a page with no background of its own showed the window's white behind its light letters in dark mode, white on white. Behind every website is now the color it would have: dark gray in dark mode, Catppuccin's base in Catppuccin for every site. A new tab doesn't flash white while its page loads anymore, in either.
- **The side panel:** a website in the side panel follows dark mode and Catppuccin for every site now, like a tab does.
- **Faster pages in Catppuccin for every site:** what a big stylesheet's rules come to (Bootstrap's, a site's own) is kept, so the next page on that site doesn't read it again. A page that uses one loads about a third faster, and Bifrost's own work on it takes about a third of the time. A stylesheet that changed is read again.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.40.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.40.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.40.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
