## Bifrost 0.36.0

- **Catppuccin for every site:** Catppuccin's own styles only cover the sites someone wrote one for (their import.json already has all of them), so most sites kept their own colors. Turn on Settings → Website styles → Catppuccin for every site and every other site gets Catppuccin's colors as it loads, from its first paint: Bifrost goes through the colors in the page's stylesheets and swaps each for the nearest of Catppuccin's. White backgrounds become base and light grays the surfaces, while a dark sidebar stays darker than the page. Text becomes Catppuccin's text and subtexts. A link's blue becomes Catppuccin's blue, an error's red its red, and a success green its green. Text stays readable (4.5 to 1 on base). It works on sites built with CSS variables (Bootstrap, Tailwind, shadcn and the rest), on colors set in the page's own HTML, on stylesheets from other sites, on styles a site's scripts add as it runs, inside web components, and in frames, including the ones rich text editors write into. Pictures stay as they are.
- **Which Catppuccin:** Mocha, or Latte where websites are light. With "Match websites to your theme" on, it's your theme's colors (Frost, Nord, Dracula…), the same as Catppuccin's own styles.
- **Your styles first:** a site that one of your styles is for (Catppuccin's own, say) keeps that style. Your own CSS for a site still goes over the new colors. If a site looks wrong, turn "Catppuccin colors" off in its info popover (the icon left of the address); Settings lists the sites you've turned it on or off for.
- **Privacy:** a page can't read stylesheets from other sites itself, so Bifrost reads them for it. It sends no cookies, accepts only CSS, and never reads from your own network (a router, a printer) for a page on the web.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.36.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.36.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.36.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
