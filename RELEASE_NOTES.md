## Bifrost 0.29.0

- **Websites in your theme:** Settings → Website styles → **Match websites to your theme**. Catppuccin's styles for websites take Bifrost's theme:
  - With one of Catppuccin's own themes (Mocha, Macchiato, Frappé, Latte), they use that flavor, and your accent if you picked one.
  - With any other theme (Frost, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox…), they're built in its colors and accent, and icons get recolored to match.
  - Pages you have open change as soon as you change the theme. With "Match the system", websites follow your computer's light or dark setting too.
  - Each style keeps its own options, like its accent, and turning the setting off puts back the colors you had.
  - To get Catppuccin's styles, download their `import.json` (Settings has a button that opens their releases page) and bring it in with **Import from Stylus**.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.29.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.29.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.29.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
