## Bifrost 0.25.1

- **A permissions shield in the address bar, in every form of it.** The site permissions button was a small sliders icon at the right end of the address bar, easy to miss, and with the compact toolbar (the title pill) it wasn't there at all. Now it's a shield at the start of the address bar, and at the start of the title pill. Click it to allow, block or reset what the site may do: notifications, camera, microphone, location and the rest. When a site was just refused something, the shield turns orange with a red dot, and its tooltip says what was blocked; one click allows it. It shows on websites only, not on Bifrost's own pages.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.1-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.1-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.1.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
