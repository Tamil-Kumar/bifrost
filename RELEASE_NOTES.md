## Bifrost 0.24.5

- **"Split view with a tab you pick" no longer freezes the window.** Run from the launcher (the command palette), it opened a second launcher while the first was closing, and the first one's closing took the second one's place: after you picked a tab, an invisible layer was left over the whole window and nothing could be clicked. The launcher now stays open and turns into the tab picker, and a launcher that's gone can't leave anything behind.
- **Enter opens a new tab, Shift+Enter this tab, Ctrl+Enter split view.** These are the new defaults, and Ctrl+Enter now adds the result to the split view the current tab is already in (up to four), instead of breaking that split up. Opened with Ctrl+T, Enter still opens in that new tab; to edit the page's address (Ctrl+L without an address bar), Enter still goes there.
- **Every key in the launcher can be changed** (Settings → Launcher):
  - **Opening results:** what Enter does with each key held (Shift, Ctrl, Alt, Ctrl+Shift, Alt+Shift, Ctrl+Alt) and what a middle-click does: this tab, a new tab, a background tab, split view, a new window, a private window, copy the address, search the web for what you typed instead, or nothing. A click does what Enter does with the same keys held.
  - **Quick open:** Alt+1…9 (or Ctrl+1…9) opens the first nine results; hold Alt to see their numbers.
  - **Keys in the launcher:** next and previous result (Down/Up, Tab, Ctrl+N/P, Ctrl+J/K), a page down or up, first and last, close, clear the box, remove the result (Shift+Delete takes it out of history, or closes the tab), copy its address (Ctrl+Shift+C), and its settings (Ctrl+,). Add, remove or reset keys; clashes are marked.
- **New launcher options:** open it with what you typed last, stay open after opening a result in a background tab (to open several), close it when you switch to another app, pointing at a result selects it (or doesn't), wrap around at the ends, how matches are marked (accent, bold, or not at all), and a button to clear the box.
- **Smaller fixes:** pressing Enter right after typing opens the result for what you typed, not the one shown a moment before (with a typing delay too); Enter that finishes a word in an input method (Chinese, Japanese, Korean…) no longer opens a result; running a command from the launcher gives the keyboard back to the page; a launcher opened under its toolbar button follows the button when the window is resized; the footer shows what each Enter does; picking a tab for split view leaves out the tabs already beside this one.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.5-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.5-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.5.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
