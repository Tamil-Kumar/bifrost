## Bifrost 0.24.8

- **The bookmarks you use come first in the launcher.** As you type, a bookmark now counts all of its use: the visits to the pages under it (your github.com/you bookmark gets the visits to your repos) and to where it sends you (a mail bookmark that opens your inbox), not just to the one address saved in it. "Most-used bookmarks", before you type, counts the same.
- **It learns from what you open.** Whatever you open from the launcher or the address bar's list counts as using it, and it comes first the next time you type the same thing (or the start of it), even over a page you visit more. Settings → Launcher → Ranking has "Learn from what you open", how much it counts, and "Forget what it learned". Private windows teach it nothing, and clearing your history clears it.
- **A permissions button in the address bar.** It shows what the site you're on may do (notifications, camera, microphone, location and the rest) and lets you change it right there. When a site asked for something and it was blocked, the button turns into that permission's icon with a red dot: click it and Allow.
- **Sites can ask to send you notifications.** A site you hadn't answered was told notifications were blocked, so sites like Claude never offered to turn them on. Now it's told you haven't decided yet, as in Chrome, so it can ask, and allowing works straight away without a reload. Clicking one of its notifications brings its tab to the front.
- **YouTube ads: not seen, not heard.** A video ad is covered and muted the moment it starts and skipped straight away, whatever kind it is (the ones stitched into the video too), so all you see is a moment of black that says "Skipping an ad…" before your video. The ads in YouTube's pages are hidden: the home feed, search, next to the video, the masthead. It works from the moment Bifrost starts, before its ad lists have loaded. YouTube itself is left as it is, so it keeps loading and doesn't complain about ad blockers.
- **No ads in YouTube Shorts.** The Shorts that are ads are taken out before YouTube shows them; one that gets through anyway is hidden, silenced and skipped.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.8-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.8-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.8.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
