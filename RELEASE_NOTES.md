## Bifrost 0.24.4

- **Match the page no longer flickers when you switch tabs.** Going from one of Bifrost's own pages to a website, the window's color used to fade in from nothing, so whatever is behind the window (Mica, on Windows) flashed through the sidebar for a moment. Switching tabs now changes the colors at once, as the page itself does; only a change of color on the same page fades.
- **Blur the page looks like frosted glass instead of smudges.** The picture behind the sidebar and toolbar is now small enough that rows of text and icons along the page's edge (YouTube's menu, say) melt into their colors instead of showing through as grey blobs, at every blur strength, with the window's fine grain over it. Switching tabs shows the new page's picture at once instead of fading over half a second.
- **Every setting of the Waypoint extension is in Settings → Launcher**, in Waypoint's order: Ranking, Sources, Boosts, Search engines, Keys & behavior, Appearance, Backup. New:
  - **Appearance:** a theme and accent of its own, font, text size, corner radius, compact density (one line per result), site icons (Bifrost's own, from Google or DuckDuckGo, or off), whether rows show addresses, bookmark folders and source labels, animations, and custom CSS for the launcher alone (Waypoint's selectors work: `.row`, `.title`, `.badge`, `#q`…).
  - **Keys & behavior:** what Enter and Shift+Enter do (this tab, a new tab, a background tab, split view), jumping to tabs that are already open, Esc clearing the box first, a typing delay, the launcher's shortcuts, opening it as a centered window, the Launcher toolbar button opening it right under the button, and whether the address bar shows the launcher's results.
  - **Sources and ranking:** only this window's tabs, how many history pages are searched instantly, where web suggestions come from, the default and keyword search engines, and the ranking sliders with Waypoint's ranges.
  - **Backup:** export and import the launcher's settings, import a settings file exported from Waypoint itself, or reset them to the defaults.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.4-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.4-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.4.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
