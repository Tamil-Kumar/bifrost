## Bifrost 0.25.3

- **Sites can tell when you've gone to another tab.** A page in a tab behind another thought it was still on screen and had your attention (Electron, which Bifrost is built on, told every tab's page that), so sites that notify you only when you're not looking, like claude.ai when Claude has finished answering, never did. Now a tab behind another is hidden to its page and doesn't have the focus, as in Chrome, and gets both back when you go back to it; so is the side panel's website while the panel is closed.
- **Notifications from sites like claude.ai show now.** Most sites send their notifications through their service worker (the part of a site that keeps running in the background), and Electron threw those away without a word while telling the site they'd gone out. Bifrost shows them itself now, as Windows notifications, with the site's picture. A click on one brings the site's tab to the front, and the site can take you to the right place (a chat, a message) as it would in Chrome, even when you click it long after it came.
- **Notifications in the portable build.** Windows only shows notifications from an app it knows, and it got to know Bifrost from the Start menu shortcut the installer makes, which BifrostPortable doesn't have, so its notifications went nowhere. Bifrost now tells Windows who it is when it starts (one registry entry, for you only; uninstalling removes it), so they show under Bifrost's name and icon in the portable build too. If one still doesn't, check that Bifrost's notifications are on in Windows' Settings → System → Notifications.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.3-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.3-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.3.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
