## Bifrost 0.24.3

- **“Match the page” works on Bifrost's own pages too** (the new tab page, Settings, History…): they become see-through, so their background runs behind the sidebar and toolbar. With a picture on the new tab page, the picture fills the whole window.
- **New: Blur the page** (Settings → Look & feel → Sidebar and toolbar, or ≡ → Layout → Sidebar and toolbar). The sidebar and toolbar become frosted glass: over a website they show a blurred picture of it, and over Bifrost's own pages they blur the background behind them. “How blurred” sets the strength.
- “Match the page” now reads the page's real colors along its edge, so pages with pictures, gradients or video there match too.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.3-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.3-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.3.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
