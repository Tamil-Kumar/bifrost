## Bifrost 0.25.5

- **Quick swipes go back and forward right away.** A two-finger swipe on the touchpad went back or forward only once the touchpad had stopped scrolling altogether, and after a quick swipe Windows keeps it coasting for a second or two after your fingers lift, so a slow swipe was quick and a fast one was slow. Now it goes the moment your fingers lift. A quick flick that hasn't quite lit the arrow still counts if the coasting right after it lights it, and what's left of the coasting doesn't start a swipe of its own on the page you went to.
- **The downloads box and a second download.** When another download started while the box was still open from the one before, it kept that one's time, ignoring how long Settings → Downloads says to show it. Each download that starts now starts that time over.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.5-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.5-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.5.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
