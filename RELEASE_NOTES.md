## Bifrost 0.25.7

- **Turning back cancels a swipe straight away, wherever your fingers are.** Swiping on past the point where the arrow lights up used to keep counting, so to take the arrow back out you had to swipe all that way back first, sometimes past where you started. Now the arrow stops counting once it's lit, so the moment you swipe the other way it starts going back out, twice as fast as it came in, and lifting your fingers before it's lit again goes nowhere. A tiny wobble as your fingers lift doesn't count as turning back. Keep going the other way and the other side's arrow comes in, as before.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.7-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.7-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.7.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
