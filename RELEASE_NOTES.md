## Bifrost 0.24.7

- **Bigger tab group dots.** The dot on a group's header is bigger in the sidebar, and with the sidebar collapsed to icons it's as big as a site's icon, so a group is easy to spot and to click. The group's line still runs from it. With horizontal tabs, a group without a name has a bigger dot too.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.7-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.7-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.7.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
