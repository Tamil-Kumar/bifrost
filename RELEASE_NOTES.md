## Bifrost 0.32.5

- **Space pauses YouTube Shorts:** Space now plays and pauses the Short on screen, as it does a video on YouTube's other pages. Shorts only hear Space while their player has the keyboard (K works from anywhere), so otherwise it landed on the page, which can't scroll, and nothing happened. Typing a space in the search box and Space on a button you've selected work as before.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.5-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.5-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.32.5.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
