## Bifrost 0.32.3

- **The mouse's Back button goes back one page:** one press could take you back two, past the page you wanted (from a search result past Google's results to the new tab page). A press of the mouse's Back button (or a keyboard's Back key) reaches Bifrost several ways at once: as Windows' "browser back" command, sometimes twice, and as a key, either the Back key or the Alt+Left that a mouse's own software sends, from the page or from Bifrost. Only one particular pair of these was being counted once. Now everything from one press counts once. Two presses within 0.4 seconds of each other also count as one; the toolbar's Back button counts every click.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.3-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.3-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.32.3.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
