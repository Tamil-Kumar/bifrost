## Bifrost 0.32.2

- **The downloads box goes when it should:** "Keep them shown for" in Settings → Downloads works again. A box that opened under the pointer (after clicking a download button near the top right of a page) thought the pointer was on it and stayed open for good: Bifrost's window doesn't hear the pointer moving over a page, so it never heard it leave. Now the box goes by where the pointer really is, and only a pointer moved onto it holds it open.
- **A close button on the downloads box,** next to the downloads folder button. It works on the box that opens by itself (also when it's set to stay until you close it) and on the one from the downloads button.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.2-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.2-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.32.2.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
