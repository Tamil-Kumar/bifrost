## Bifrost 0.24.6

- **Tab groups have a line through all their tabs, like Chrome.** In the sidebar, a line in the group's color comes out of the group's dot and runs down beside every tab in the group, collapsed to icons too (where there was no line at all before). With horizontal tabs, one line runs under the group's label and all of its tabs. The line is brighter for the group with the tab you're on, and goes away while the group is collapsed.
- **Dragging tabs no longer glitches.** When you let go, the tab flew down and to the right (past where it was going) and then snapped back into place. That's because the animation style made its glide 1.7 times longer (2.4 times with Playful). It now glides straight into its spot. The dragged tab also stays exactly under the pointer: before, it drifted away from it the further you dragged.
- **The dragged tab looks like the tab you picked up.** With the sidebar collapsed to icons (or for a pinned tab), it's the icon tile, not a stretched tab with its × floating outside the sidebar. With horizontal tabs, it has the shape of a horizontal tab.
- **Smaller fixes:** with the sidebar collapsed, the page no longer turns into a split-view drop zone as soon as the pointer slips a few pixels off the sidebar. A dropped tab never shows for a moment in its old place. With horizontal tabs, the spot a tab will land in is the height of a tab.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.6-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.6-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.6.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
