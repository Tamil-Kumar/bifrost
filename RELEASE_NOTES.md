## Bifrost 0.25.2

- **Switch to links you open in a new tab.** Ctrl+click or middle-click on a link opens it in a new tab behind the one you're on (as in Chrome). Turn on **Settings → Tabs → Switch to links you open in a new tab** and they take you there instead (as in Firefox); with Shift held too, they open behind. **Open link in new tab** in a link's menu follows it too. A link that opens a tab by itself always comes to the front, either way.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.2-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.2-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.2.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
