## Bifrost 0.32.1

- **Settings' sidebar scrolls on its own:** when it's taller than the window, the scroll wheel over it moves the sidebar, not the page, so its bottom sections are always in reach. Before, it stayed put while Settings scrolled, and its last sections were out of reach until you got to the very end of Settings. As you scroll Settings, the section you're on stays in sight in the sidebar.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.1-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.32.1-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.32.1.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
