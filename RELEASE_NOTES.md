## Bifrost 0.25.0

- **Websites and extensions in the side panel, with a side bar like Firefox's.** With tabs on top, a column of buttons runs down the left edge of the window; with tabs on the side, they're a row at the bottom of the sidebar. There's one for Bookmarks, History and Downloads, and for each website and extension you put there: click it to open it in the side panel, click again to close it.
  - **Websites:** the side bar's **+** adds the page you're on, any address, or one of a few suggestions (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Translate). You can also right-click a tab → **Add to the side panel**. They're signed in like your tabs and keep running while the window is open, so a chat stays where you left it. They work as they do in a tab: they can ask to show notifications (the question shows under the panel's title), a click on one of their notifications opens them in the side panel, their links open as tabs, and unread counts like WhatsApp's "(3)" show on their button. The panel's title row has Back, Reload, Open in a new tab and a menu (Edit, Move up or down, Put to sleep, Remove).
  - **Extensions:** ones with a side panel of their own (TickTick) are on the side bar as before. Now an extension that only has a popup (Bitwarden, say) can go there too, from the **+**: its popup page stays open in the side panel instead of closing when you click away.
  - **Settings → Side panel** turns the side bar off (the panels are then tabs at the top of the side panel, as before), hides any of its buttons, and adds or removes websites and extensions.
- **No empty space above the side panel.** It goes from the top of the window to the bottom, next to the tabs; before, it started below the toolbar.
- **Choose how long the downloads box stays.** It opens when a download starts and closed after 5 seconds. Now **Settings → Downloads → Keep them shown for** sets anything from 1 to 60 seconds, or until you close it. While the pointer is over it, it stays.
- **A list of your tabs while you hold Ctrl+Tab, like Chrome's.** Turn on **Settings → Tabs → Show a list of your tabs while Ctrl is held**. Then hold Ctrl and press Tab: your tabs show in a list, Tab goes down it and Shift+Tab up, and letting go of Ctrl switches to the one picked (or click one). Esc stays where you are. A quick Ctrl+Tab still switches straight away, without the list.
- **Fixed: Ctrl+Tab after closing a tab with Ctrl+W.** A quick Ctrl+Tab should go back to the tab you used before; after Ctrl+W (or another Ctrl shortcut a page had the keyboard for) it went on to the next tab instead, because Bifrost never heard Ctrl come up.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
