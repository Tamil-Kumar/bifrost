## Bifrost 0.25.4

- **Arrange the side bar, + included.** The side bar's buttons (Bookmarks, History, Downloads, your websites and extensions, its + and its settings button) now have their own row in **Settings → Toolbar & sidebar**, under the toolbar's and the sidebar's: drag them into the order you like, take any of them off (the + too), put them back from the tray under it, and add flexible spaces. A right-click on the + (or the settings button) takes it off right there, and **Customize the side bar…** in any of its right-click menus gets you to the row. With the + off, a right-click on the side bar or on a tab still adds a website. Websites can be taken off the side bar and brought back without removing them, too.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.4-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.4-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.4.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
