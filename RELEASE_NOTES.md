## Bifrost 0.33.0

- **Pages load faster:** three things were slowing every page down. Since 0.32.0, every tab had YouTube's ad removal watching its requests, whatever site it was on; now only tabs on YouTube do (a link that sends you on to YouTube included). Every page, and every frame in it, stopped to ask Bifrost several questions, one after another, before it could start (its user agent, your styles for it, its notification permission, what to hide); now a page asks once and its frames don't stop at all. And Bifrost read the headers of every answer a page got; now only those of pages themselves. On a test page with 200 requests, loading took about a fifth less time (0.88 s to 0.69 s on the test machine).
- **Bifrost opens faster:** the window is on screen about half a second sooner (it used to wait for everything behind it to be set up), the window's own scripts are fetched together instead of one after another, and the launcher's index of your history is built once Bifrost is open instead of before. The first page is in a little sooner too (about 5% on the test machine).
- **“Match the page” on bright pages:** with Look & feel → Sidebar and toolbar → Match the page, a bright white page next to a dark theme, or a neon or fully saturated one, turned the whole window that color: it glared, and the tabs, the address and the icons were hard to read on it (dark red on red, pale yellow on white). Now a loud color is calmed for the sidebar and toolbar: white becomes a soft light gray next to a dark theme, a vivid color a pastel of itself, and a color neither black nor white reads on a darker shade of itself (a lighter one with a light theme). Text on them is always readable (7:1 for tab titles on every page color with every theme), and warnings, folders and tab group colors are darkened or lightened where they wouldn't show. Calm page colors, dark pages included, are taken exactly as before.
- **New setting, Look & feel → Bright and vivid pages:** “Soften it” (the above), “Tint my theme” (the sidebar and toolbar stay as dark or light as your theme, with a touch of the page's color) or “Exactly the page's” (its color whatever it is, as before).

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.33.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.33.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.33.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
