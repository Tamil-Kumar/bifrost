## Bifrost 0.25.8

- **The buttons at the bottom of the sidebar are evenly spaced.** The side bar's buttons (Downloads, your websites…) and the sidebar's bottom row sat in two separate blocks with a line and extra room between them, so in the collapsed sidebar the gap after the last side bar button was wider than the rest. Now they're one block under one line, every button the same distance from the next, collapsed or open. In the collapsed sidebar, the spaces buttons are the same size as the others, and turning the spaces strip off no longer leaves a gap where it was.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.8-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.8-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.8.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
