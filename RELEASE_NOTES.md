## Bifrost 0.24.2

- **The sidebar and toolbar can blend into the page:** Settings → Look & feel → Sidebar and toolbar → Match the page (or ≡ → Layout). They take the color of the page you're on, with no edge or shadow between them and it, so the page and the window read as one. Text stays readable on light and dark pages; Bifrost's own pages keep your theme.
- **Fixed:** after closing the tab you were on, the title pill, Back and Forward, the bookmark star and extension buttons kept showing the closed page. On a new tab page the pill now says “New tab”.
- **Fixed:** with tabs on top, tabs never got narrower, so with many open the first ones slid off the window. They now shrink to fit, scroll only once they're as narrow as they get, and a new tab scrolls into view.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.2-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.2-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.2.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
