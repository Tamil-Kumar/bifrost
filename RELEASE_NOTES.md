## Bifrost 0.24.1

- **Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video play:** this version is signed for Widevine. Bifrost 0.24.0 updates to it by itself; this once it's the whole build (the signature comes with Bifrost.exe), not just Bifrost's own files.
- From 0.24.0: **Bifrost updates itself** (the ≡ menu, and Settings → Default browser & about → Updates, offer Restart to update); protected video and music through castlabs' Electron with Google's Widevine (the first start downloads it, a few MB); Bitwarden signs in and fills. Since 0.22: sync between your computers through a cloud folder (Settings → Sync), page translation, warnings before dangerous sites.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.1-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.24.1-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.24.1.zip` — unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays).
