## Bifrost 0.38.0

- **Download progress in the side panel:** each download that's still going shows a bar of how far along it is, the percentage beside it, and how much has come, how fast and how long is left. When a site doesn't say how big the file is, the bar moves back and forth without a percentage.
- **Cancel and Restart:** a download that's going has a Cancel button. One that's going, cancelled or failed has a Restart button, which downloads it again from the beginning and replaces the old entry (no cancelled copy left behind).

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.38.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.38.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.38.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
