## Bifrost 0.38.1

- **Remove from downloads:** every download in the side panel has a trash button that takes it out of the list. A finished download's file stays where it is; one that's still going is cancelled first, and what it had of the file goes with it.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.38.1-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.38.1-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.38.1.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
