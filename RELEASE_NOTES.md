## Bifrost 0.39.2

- **Document previews:** previews of documents stayed white on dark pages: the templates and your recent documents on Docs' home page, Drive's, and the attachments on a Classroom assignment. In dark mode and in Catppuccin's dark flavors they're dark paper now, with light text, and their colors kept (a table's blue header stays blue). Bifrost only does this on Google's apps, and only for pictures that look like a page of a document; photos are left alone.
- **Classroom's white corner:** the top-left corner of an assignment's page stayed white in dark mode. Classroom draws that rounded corner as a small shape in its page's white, which Chromium's dark mode never darkens; Bifrost darkens it now. In Catppuccin for every site it had gone light too; now it matches the page around it.
- **Docs' toolbar icons, once more:** icons in a toolbar that shows up after the page has loaded (Docs builds its toolbar late) are lightened when they appear, instead of being missed while they were hidden. Docs' toolbar icons were already fixed in 0.39.1, which installs the next time Bifrost starts; if your toolbar still has grey icons, restart Bifrost.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.39.2-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.39.2-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.39.2.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
