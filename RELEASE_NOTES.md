## Bifrost 0.34.0

- **“Match the page” next to bright pages, done again:** 0.33.0 turned the sidebar and toolbar a light gray next to a white page (and a dusty pastel next to a colored one), which looked washed out next to a dark theme. Now, with a dark theme, they stay dark next to a bright page: your theme's own shade with a touch of the page's color, with the page in its usual frame. Dark pages still give them their exact color, with no edge between. A vivid color in between (a full red, a strong blue) gives them a deeper shade of itself, still as vivid, instead of a faded one.
- **Bright pages setting:** Look & feel → Bright pages now has “Stay dark” (the above, the default), “Go light” (they take the page's color, with your light theme's text, accent and colors, so a white page looks like your light theme) and “Exactly the page's”. “Tint my theme” from 0.33.0 is now part of “Stay dark”.
- **Colors stay colors:** where a warning's orange, a folder's yellow or your accent had to change to show on the bars, they were mixed toward black or white and came out muddy. They're now made deeper or lighter in the same color.
- **Tab titles:** a title too long for its tab fades out at the end instead of ending in faded dots, and uses the room of the close button until you point at the tab.
- **Dropdowns:** the system's heavy arrow is replaced with the same thin chevron as the rest of Bifrost's icons, in Settings and in the site information and bookmark boxes.
- **Light themes:** a switch that's off has a white knob on a gray track (the dark knob read as on), and plain buttons are light with an edge instead of gray, which read as switched off.
- **New tab page:** shortcuts are centered under the search box (a row that isn't full sat to its left).
- **History:** the day's name is a small frosted label that stays in view (it was a dark box across the page), and History and Downloads use the full width of their header.
- **Smaller things:** rows in the site information box have room between them, the three-dot buttons are easier to see, and the scrollbar of Settings' side navigation shows only while you're using it.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.34.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.34.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.34.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
