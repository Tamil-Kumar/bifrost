## Bifrost 0.25.6

- **Swipes go back and forward only when your fingers really leave the touchpad.** 0.25.5 took a pause in the touchpad's scrolling for your fingers lifting, and Windows sends those while your fingers are still down too, so a swipe could go back or forward in the middle of moving your fingers about. Now Bifrost asks the touchpad itself: a small helper reads how many fingers are on a precision touchpad (the kind almost every Windows laptop has; a palm resting on it doesn't count), so a swipe goes the moment they lift, and never while they're down, however long you hold them there or move them back and forth. With another kind of touchpad it waits for the end of the whole scroll, as before 0.25.5.
- **The arrow follows your fingers.** Swiping back the other way takes the arrow out, and on past where you started brings in the other side's (forward instead of back), as in Chrome; lifting goes wherever the lit arrow says. A quick flick that hasn't quite lit the arrow still counts if the touchpad's coasting right after it lights it, and the coasting never brings in the other arrow.
- **Two swipes in a row.** Putting your fingers back on the touchpad while it's still coasting from a swipe starts the next one straight away, so going back two pages is just two quick swipes.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.6-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.25.6-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.25.6.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
