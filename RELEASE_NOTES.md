## Bifrost 0.39.0

- **Google Docs in the dark:** Docs draws its pages on a canvas, which stayed white in dark mode (or showed black letters on a dark page). Its pages are dark now with light text, in dark mode for websites and in Catppuccin for every site; links keep their color.
- **Icons that vanished:** Google draws most of its icons as black shapes in pictures, or as masks over a dark gray, and they disappeared on dark pages (Gmail's sidebar, Docs' toolbar). Catppuccin for every site now draws them in text's color, and dark mode lightens the mask ones.
- **Logos and pictures left alone:** photos and logos keep their colors (an SVG in three colors or more, like Google's logo and microphone, isn't turned into Catppuccin's); white on a one-color icon (Drive's file icons) stays readable; text over a picture (a Classroom banner) keeps its own color, in Latte too.
- **No more half-dark pages:** frames from other sites (Google's account card in Gmail, an embedded form) stayed white in dark mode; they're dark now. In Catppuccin for every site, text and icons that still didn't read on what's behind them are fixed.
- **Your styles stay up to date:** styles that say where they come from (Catppuccin's do) are updated once a day, so they keep up when Google changes its pages. Settings → Website styles → Keep your styles up to date.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.39.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.39.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.39.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
