## Bifrost 0.35.0

- **The window works while Bifrost is still starting:** when a start takes long (the first one, while Widevine for protected video is still being fetched: up to four seconds), the window was there but empty until everything was ready. Now its tabs, sidebar and toolbar are there at once, and Settings, History and the new tab page open right away; web pages start as soon as Bifrost has (they need the ad blocker's lists and Widevine from their first request). A normal start takes as long as before.
- **“Blur the page” next to bright pages:** with a dark theme, a bright page's blurred picture behind the sidebar and toolbar glared and left their text hard to read, as “Match the page” did before 0.34.0. Now it's under dark glass there, with the page's light and colors glowing through, and the page in its frame. Dark pages look as before. Look & feel → Bright pages applies to it too (“Go light” and “Exactly the page's” keep the bright picture).
- **YouTube's ad blocking keeps itself up to date:** how YouTube's ads are taken out (which of its answers, which names in them, what's hidden and pressed in its pages) is now a small list that Bifrost fetches from its repository a minute after it starts and every six hours, and with Settings → Privacy & security → Update filter lists now. When YouTube changes something, the fix can reach you the same day, in tabs already open too, without a new version of Bifrost. A list with anything in it your version doesn't know is refused, and the one you have stays.
- **Small windows:** with the sidebar open in a narrow window, the toolbar ran past the window's edge: the window's own buttons were pushed out of it (or, where Windows draws them, the menu ended up under them), and the star was drawn over the next button. Now the buttons that matter least step aside until it fits (extension icons first, Back last), and the window's buttons, the menu and the address bar always stay. They're back when there's room.
- **Bifrost's own pages in small windows:** headers go to a second row instead of squeezing (“Clear browsing data…” was on three lines), a setting's control goes under its description instead of leaving it a column a few words wide, the Extensions page no longer scrolls sideways, and the new tab page's search box keeps its text on one line.
- **Faint text you can read:** shortcuts in menus, placeholders and the lines under a setting's name were under 3:1 against their background in Nord, Tokyo Night, Catppuccin Latte and Frappé, and Frost Light. They're a shade stronger there (the same color); the other themes are unchanged.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.35.0-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.35.0-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.35.0.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
