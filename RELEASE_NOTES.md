## Bifrost 0.35.1

- **“Match the page” you can see:** a page in the middle of the range of lightness (a steel blue, a mid gray, a purple, a full red) gave the sidebar and toolbar its own color, and most sites' icons and the theme's colors are mid-tones too, made to show on white and on dark gray: on a steel blue they were at 2 to 1 or so against it, and hard to make out. Now the bars are always dark or light, never in between. A dark page still gives them its color as it is; a page in the middle gives them a deep shade of its color, with the page in its frame (with a light theme, a pale shade, or a deep one for a dark blue). On a steel blue page, an orange site icon goes from 2.2 to 1 to 4.2 to 1, and a light blue one from 2.7 to 1 to 5.1 to 1.
- **Pages whose edge is a picture or a gradient:** a header picture fading into the page (as on Tabroom) has no one color along the edge, and the color picked was whichever covered the most of it, often from its middle, matching nothing. Now the bars take the color where they meet the page, and the page keeps its frame. While the page is still being looked at, the bars no longer flash to the colors meant for a white page.
- **Text on the bars:** secondary and faint text (the lines under a tab group's name, shortcuts, the toolbar's quieter icons) and the theme's colors on them are held to more contrast than before.
- **Light themes on dark pages:** with a light theme and a dark page, the active tab and the address bar turned white while their text stayed light, so you couldn't read them. They now match the dark bars.

**Install:** `Bifrost-Setup-0.35.1-x64.exe` for most PCs, `Bifrost-Setup-0.35.1-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops (Snapdragon). It installs for you only, no administrator rights needed, and keeps your profile. **Portable:** `BifrostPortable-0.35.1.zip`: unzip it over your BifrostPortable folder (its Data folder stays). Bifrost 0.24.0 and later update themselves.
